1. Extract this WHOLE folder (e.g. C:\JarvisPrimez). Do not run from inside the zip.
2. Double-click JarvisPrimez.exe  (SmartScreen: More info > Run anyway).
3. Settings: paste your Gemini key, set your Home city. Optional: a free Groq key (2nd AI + Whisper speech recognition).
4. Say "Hi Primez" then try:
   "Play Believer by Imagine Dragons" / "pause" / "next" / "volume up"
   "Remind me to call mom in 10 minutes" / "Add milk to my shopping list"
   "Remember that my birthday is June 5" / "Good morning" (daily briefing)
   "What's the weather" / "What's the news" / "Make a presentation about space"
Files are saved in Documents\JarvisPrimez and Pictures\JarvisPrimez. Your reminders, lists and notes are in the clock/list panel.
This folder contains your Google OAuth config - do not upload it publicly.
